# Farm D-CORE v5 scientific contract

This document freezes the MAS-side construction. Agronomic predicates,
tolerances, world branches, deadlines, negative-action definitions, and role
refinements become usable in paper mode only after independent domain review
confirms their digest.

## Foundations and bounded novelty

The contract deliberately builds on established objects rather than renaming
them. Execution order follows Lamport's happens-before relation and vector
timestamps; concurrency is represented with occurrence-net/event-structure
semantics; the legacy process-mining comparison is PM4Py token replay; and
claims of actual cause are reserved for controlled counterfactual contrasts in
the spirit of Halpern--Pearl causality. Relevant foundations are:

- Lamport, *Time, Clocks, and the Ordering of Events in a Distributed System*:
  <https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/time-clocks-ordering-events-distributed-system/>
- Winskel, *Event Structures*:
  <https://doi.org/10.7146/dpb.v18i278.6655>
- Halpern and Pearl, *Causes and Explanations: A Structural-Model Approach*:
  <https://doi.org/10.1093/bjps/axi147>
- Partially ordered conformance checking:
  <https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/conformance-checking-based-on-partially-ordered-event-data>
- PM4Py conformance interfaces:
  <https://github.com/process-intelligence-solutions/pm4py/blob/release/docs/source/api.rst>

Adjacent multi-agent benchmarks and taxonomies already measure milestones,
communication behavior, or annotated failure classes (for example
MultiAgentBench and MAST). Farm D-CORE's bounded contribution is their missing
intersection: black-box long-horizon farm agents, exact time-indexed local
knowledge and fact-version provenance, concurrency-aware normative
conformance, planted communication faults, and paired delayed physical
outcomes. The implementation therefore does **not** claim that vector clocks
establish physical causality, that Petri notation is itself novel, or that a
single trace identifies an actual cause.

## Four distinct relations

Farm D-CORE keeps four objects separate.

1. **Execution happens-before** is generated by actor program order, message
   send-to-receive, and recorded world-effect parents. Vector clocks encode
   this relation; they do not establish normative or physical cause.
2. **Normative dependence** is the reviewed set of workflow, evidence,
   freshness, resource, and scope obligations in the occurrence net.
3. **Information state** is the set of fact-version IDs in the immutable
   pre-decision snapshot. It excludes undelivered and authoritative hidden
   truth.
4. **Physical outcome** is FarmARE's authoritative state and biological and
   marketable yield. A causal effect on outcome may be claimed only from a
   paired controlled intervention, never from trace reachability alone.

The process is a hierarchical, data-aware farm workflow compiled
deterministically into a labeled 1-safe occurrence net. This is deliberately a
narrower claim than implementing arbitrary timed or data Petri nets.

## Non-anticipating policies and branches

Every information policy is an exhaustive, disjoint table:

```text
fact-verdict vector × deadline state × channel state
    -> permitted responses, required responses
```

Each fact verdict is `TRUE`, `FALSE`, or `UNKNOWN`; `ANY` is available only in
the specification pattern. The validator enumerates the finite state space and
rejects gaps or overlaps. The evaluator independently reconstructs the fact
vector, freshness, supersession, deadline, channel state, and selected rule
from the saved trace. Runtime policy commitments are audit records, never
metric input.

World branches are selected only from timestamped authoritative fact versions
available at the declared commitment phase. The evaluator independently
recomputes the unique branch. A missing, ambiguous, future-dependent, or
runtime-only selection invalidates paper eligibility.

## Event and obligation profiles

For every required transition, expert-reviewed categorical predicates cover
owner/tool, arguments, scope, timing, and successful execution. A transition
is fully conforming only when every critical predicate passes. There is no soft
numeric decay. Presence and each conditional acceptance rate are reported
separately.

Unmatched actions are evaluated against frozen world-predicate classifiers and
reported as `prohibited`, `unnecessary`, `benign`, or `unresolved`. Runtime
"harmful" labels are ignored. Negative penalties come only from explicit
reviewed obligations.

Each causal-conformance item is one end-to-end semantic obligation. Refining a
communication link into more hops changes its diagnostics, not its weight or
score denominator. An obligation passes when one predeclared realization has
all required happens-before edges and fact guards. Missing target actions are
charged by event fidelity; modules with no applicable obligation report `NA`.

Module-normalized `EF` and `CC` are primary. The scalar

```text
DCORE = 0.5 EF + 0.5 CC
```

is secondary, with predeclared 0.25/0.5/0.75 sensitivity. No scalar weight is
tuned against final yield.

## Local/global discordance

For each applicable high-impact decision `d`, the evaluator reconstructs:

- `L(d)=1` when the response follows the policy implied by the actor's actual
  information state;
- `G(d)=1` when the response/action conforms to authoritative truth and the
  reviewed occurrence net.

It reports the weighted 2-by-2 table and

```text
IGD = sum_d w_d 1[L(d)=1 and G(d)=0] / sum_d w_d
```

Per-role policy conformance remains descriptive. Zero-obligation actors are
`NA`. The former scalar `LocalDCORE` and denominator-mismatched `LGG` are not
primary v5 results.

## Provenance failure localization

The evaluator follows exact identifiers:

```text
authoritative fact version -> observation version -> claim -> delivery
-> forwarded version -> decision snapshot -> physical action
```

It reports the earliest recorded broken link. This is provenance-based failure
localization, not actual causation. Structural downstream reach and
remaining-horizon exposure are descriptive. Yield effects require paired
fault/enforcement interventions with the same exogenous-world digest. Free text
is unverifiable unless a frozen annotation maps it to fact versions.

## Frozen estimands and analysis

- representation: free text versus causal audit under reliable transport;
- enforcement: causal enforce versus causal audit under the same fault;
- partial information: local causal audit versus shared blackboard;
- fault effect: each active fault versus reliable transport within one
  representation;
- team size: a separate bounded 2/3/4-agent scalability block.

One season is one observational unit. Primary cells use ten world seeds and two
controller repeats; secondary blocks use five seeds. Comparisons are blocked by
scenario/world/repeat, intervals use world-cluster bootstrap, and Holm correction
is applied within predeclared contrast families. Primary summaries are
intention-to-treat. Metric-yield calibration is held-out or
leave-one-world-cluster-out and is interpreted as predictive validity only.

## Paper eligibility

Only `farm_process_spec_v5`, `dcore_trace_v5`, and `dcore_eval_v5` enter paper
aggregation. Paper mode requires a frozen and confirmed process digest,
reviewed world branches and deadlines, all eight named fault treatments, an
active fault manifestation, and matching team/refinement review where
applicable. PM4Py 2.7.23.4 token replay on a deterministic sequential
projection is retained as a limited legacy baseline; it is not a D-CORE
component.
