# Declared combined drought development design

This is a new author-defined benchmark candidate, not a correction of historical
data. The isolated dry-spell factor did not deplete the original 1.45 m root
layer; the isolated 0.4 m root-depth factor did not create drought across worlds
0–4. The next development check combines these already specified factors:

- Existing `calibration_candidate=true`: a 38-day dry spell beginning July 10.
- Existing `drought_rootzone_v2`: a 0.4 m effective root layer on ridges 20–43.
- Existing 3.2-hour irrigation dose and 6 field-mm seasonal quota.
- Existing three-week whole-season harvest retry cap, with every native rejection.

The dry spell is an experimental environmental treatment; restricted roots are
an explicit soil modeling assumption. Neither is claimed as a measurement from
a real farm. Check all development worlds 0–4, preserve every outcome, and apply
the unchanged accepted-intervention, complete-pair, 50% stress and 1% omission-loss
criteria. No new root depth, dose, reward, stress threshold or harvest-cap search
is part of this check. Confirmation worlds remain unused until the candidate,
specifications and sensitivity alternatives are frozen.
